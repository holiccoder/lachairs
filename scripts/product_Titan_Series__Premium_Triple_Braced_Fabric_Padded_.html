    Static vs. Dynamic Weight Ratings

    Static

    Dynamic

    Definition

    The static weight rating refers to the maximum weight the chair can hold when the load is applied gradually and remains stationary.

    The dynamic weight rating indicates the maximum weight the chair can withstand when subjected to a sudden impact or movement.

    Test Method

    The test involves evenly distributing weight onto the chair, and maintaining it in a stationary position.

    This rating is determined by a "drop test," where a weight is dropped onto the chair from a height of 6 inches above the seat.

    Purpose

    To measure the chair’s ability to support a constant, stationary load without failing.

    To assess the chair’s durability and strength when exposed to sudden, impact forces, simulating real-world use.
